Yes — cancel anytime from your billing settings. You'll keep access until the end of your current billing period. No cancellation fees or long-term contracts.
Do I need my own Anthropic API key? +
On the Growth and Enterprise plans, ARS Engine handles the Anthropic API key — you don't need your own. On the free Starter plan, you can optionally bring your own key to unlock AI features.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about ARS Engine, ARS scoring, and how to get the most from the platform.

Categories

General

What is ARS Engine? +
ARS Engine is a PE-grade acquisition intelligence platform. It takes a raw list of companies (from SaaSquatch, Apollo, or any CSV) and scores every company across 5 acquisition readiness dimensions, runs live web signal scraping, and generates Claude AI analysis including an acquisition thesis and tailored outreach email for each target.
What CSV formats does ARS Engine support? +
ARS Engine supports exports from SaaSquatch Leads, Apollo.io, Hunter.io, ZoomInfo, Crunchbase, and any generic company CSV. We auto-normalize headers across 20+ export formats — you just need a column that contains company names.
Do I need to set up a backend to use ARS Engine? +
No. The heuristic scoring engine runs entirely in your browser — zero setup, zero API keys, instant results. The optional FastAPI backend unlocks live web scraping and Claude AI analysis, but you can score 20+ companies in demo mode right now with no installation required.
Which industries does ARS Engine cover? +
ARS Engine covers all industries but is specifically calibrated for the types of businesses that PE searchers and ETA operators typically target: IT Services, HealthTech, Industrial Manufacturing, Logistics, Financial Services, Professional Services, and similar SMB sectors. The ARS scoring weights reflect acquisition probability in these sectors specifically.
What countries are supported? +
ARS Engine works with companies from any country, but the scoring is calibrated for US, UK, Canada, and Australia markets. Exit signal detection (COO job postings, succession signals) is most accurate for English-language web content. International market support is on our roadmap for H2 2025.

Scoring & AI

How accurate is the ARS scoring? +
Heuristic scoring achieves ~75% accuracy vs manual analyst assessment. When Claude AI enrichment is enabled with live web scraping, accuracy rises to ~94%. We recommend using ARS for initial prioritization, then applying human judgment to Tier S and A targets before advancing to outreach.
What does each ARS dimension measure? +
Owner Exit Signal (30%): detects founder succession readiness via age signals, COO job postings, tenure, and succession language. Financial Health (25%): revenue range, growth signals, hiring trends. Operational Leverage (20%): systematization, team depth, tech stack maturity. Market Tailwinds (15%): industry growth/contraction context. Competitive Moat (10%): niche positioning, customer concentration risk.
What is a Tier S, A, B, or C company? +
Tier S (score ≥78): Acquire Now — strong exit signals, financial health, and operational readiness. Immediate outreach priority. Tier A (score 60-77): Strong Fit — solid acquisition candidate, worth a first call this quarter. Tier B (score 42-59): Monitor — potential fit, check back in 6 months. Tier C (score <42): Not Ready — deprioritize, likely not in a sellable position.
Does ARS Engine require an Anthropic API key? +
The heuristic scoring engine runs with zero API keys. To unlock Claude AI analysis and live web scraping, the platform connects to the Anthropic API. On Growth and Enterprise plans, this is handled for you. On the free Starter plan, you can optionally bring your own key to unlock AI features at no additional cost beyond Anthropic's usage fees.

Pricing & Plans

What counts as one "ARS score"? +
One ARS score = one company scored. Re-scoring a cached company (within 24 hours) doesn't consume a credit. AI analysis (Claude) is included in all paid plans — each company analyzed counts as one score.

Is there a free trial for the Growth plan? +
Yes — all new accounts get a 14-day free trial of Growth features, no credit card required. After 14 days, you'll stay on the Starter plan unless you upgrade.

Data & Privacy

Where does ARS Engine get its data? +
ARS Engine only scores data you upload. We don't maintain a proprietary database of companies. Web signal scraping (live, async) queries public web pages, job boards, and business directories to enrich the companies you provide. No private or confidential data is accessed.
Is my data stored or shared? +
Company data you upload is stored encrypted in Supabase (Postgres) for the duration of your session. AI analysis results are cached in Redis for 24 hours to avoid redundant API calls. We never share your deal pipeline or target companies with third parties. Full details in our Privacy Policy.
Is ARS Engine GDPR compliant? +
Yes. ARS Engine is GDPR compliant. All data is processed in accordance with GDPR requirements. EU customers can request data deletion at any time. We have a Data Processing Agreement (DPA) available for Enterprise customers on request.
