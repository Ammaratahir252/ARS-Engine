ARS Signal Detection

Roofing-specific acquisition signals ARS detects

ARS Engine is trained on roofing industry patterns — not generic business data. Each signal is weighted for acquisition probability in this vertical.

👤
Aging Owner / Founder
Roofing business owners are among the hardest-working in home services — most are eager to exit by 60. ARS flags founders using LinkedIn, founding year, and tenure signals.
⛈️
Storm Revenue Mix
Roofers with balanced revenue between insurance restoration and standard replacement trade at higher multiples than pure storm chasers. ARS scores revenue durability accordingly.
📋
Project Manager Postings
Job postings for Project Manager, Production Manager, or Estimator Lead signal an owner delegating operations — a reliable pre-exit behavior pattern in roofing companies.
🏢
Commercial Roofing Mix
Roofers with commercial accounts (flat roofs, industrial, multi-family) benefit from larger contract sizes and repeat maintenance revenue. ARS identifies commercial mix from job reviews and bid history.
📝
Insurance Claim Expertise
Roofers with established insurance adjuster relationships and documented claim approval rates signal a differentiated market position that buyers can leverage at scale.
📍
Hail & Storm Corridor
ARS identifies roofing operators in high-frequency hail and storm markets (TX, CO, MN, IL) where demand is structurally elevated — improving post-acquisition revenue predictability.
