ARS Signal Detection

Landscaping-specific acquisition signals ARS detects

ARS Engine is trained on landscaping industry patterns — not generic business data. Each signal is weighted for acquisition probability in this vertical.

👤
Aging Owner / Founder
Landscaping business owners typically exit between 55–65. ARS flags founders approaching exit age using LinkedIn, founding year data, and years-in-business signals.
📜
Commercial Contract Mix
Landscapers with 40%+ commercial accounts (HOAs, municipalities, office parks) trade at premium multiples due to contract revenue stability and multi-year retention rates.
📋
Crew Supervisor Postings
Job postings for Crew Lead, Field Supervisor, or Operations Coordinator indicate an owner trying to step back from daily operations — a classic pre-exit transition signal.
🌿
Full-Service Capability
Operators offering mowing, irrigation, hardscaping, and snow removal command higher multiples than seasonal mow-only businesses. ARS identifies service breadth from reviews and web content.
🚛
Fleet & Equipment Age
Landscapers with aging fleets (10+ year old equipment) and no recent CapEx signal a coasting owner — often a motivated seller who hasn't reinvested in years.
📍
Suburban Growth Corridors
ARS identifies landscapers operating in fast-growing suburban markets where new housing starts and HOA formation drive organic demand growth post-acquisition.
