ARS Signal Detection

Plumbing-specific acquisition signals ARS detects

ARS Engine is trained on plumbing industry patterns — not generic business data. Each signal is weighted for acquisition probability in this vertical.

👤
Aging Owner / Founder
Detects founders 55+ based on LinkedIn profile age, founding year, and business tenure. Plumbing owners average 57 at exit — ARS flags them early before they list with a broker.
📋
Operations Manager Postings
Job postings for Dispatch Manager, Service Coordinator, or Operations Lead are the clearest pre-exit signal in residential services. ARS monitors these in real time.
🔄
Recurring Service Contracts
Plumbing businesses with active maintenance contracts, annual inspection agreements, or commercial retainer clients show predictable cash flow — a key driver of acquisition multiple.
🏆
Multi-Trade Capability
Plumbers who also handle gas lines, water heaters, and drain cleaning trade at a premium vs. single-service operators. ARS identifies service breadth from job descriptions and reviews.
🔧
Legacy Dispatch Systems
Businesses using manual dispatch or outdated field service software (pre-2020 versions of ServiceTitan or Jobber) signal operational improvement opportunity post-acquisition.
📍
Geographic Route Density
ARS identifies plumbing operators in suburban and exurban markets where route consolidation adds immediate margin — a key value creation lever in home services roll-ups.
